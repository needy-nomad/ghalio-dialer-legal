Ghalio Dialer privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · applies to the Android app "Ghalio Dialer" (com.ghalio.dialer)

Summary

Ghalio Dialer does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything it handles stays on your phone. There are no ads, accounts, analytics or trackers.

What the app uses on your phone, and why

  • Phone calls, call state and call history: to work as your phone app (placing, answering and showing calls, call quality/location details, and your call history).
  • Contacts: to display caller names/organization details and decide whether a caller is unknown so automatic filter/phrase rules can run.
  • Microphone: used on-device for the real-time Live Voice Filter during active calls when selected, as well as recording local preview samples for voice effects. Audio never leaves the device and calls are never recorded or stored.
  • Speech: your phrases in "Speak for me" are turned into speech by the speech engine already on your phone, using voices that work offline. Phrases and generated speech are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Network (local Wireless debugging): Android restricts ordinary apps from injecting audio directly into a cellular call, so Ghalio Dialer starts its local on-device audio helper through your phone's own Wireless debugging (`127.0.0.1`), which you pair once. The app has the internet permission only to connect locally on the phone itself; no data is ever sent to any external device or server.
  • Shizuku (optional): if you choose to use Shizuku instead, Ghalio Dialer uses it strictly on your phone in the same way.
  • Notifications: for the ongoing-call notification, pairing prompts, and setup status alerts.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, including the developer. Call audio is never recorded or uploaded.

Your choices

You can deny any permission in Android settings; features that need it stop working. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
